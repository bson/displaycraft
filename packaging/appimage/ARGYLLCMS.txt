ArgyllCMS in this AppImage

The programs in usr/lib/argyll are a modified ArgyllCMS 3.5.0 (by Graeme W.
Gill, https://www.argyllcms.com), licensed under the GNU Affero General
Public License version 3 (ArgyllCMS-LICENSE.txt).

It was modified to run natively on Wayland under KDE Plasma (KWin), as well
as on X11, choosing when each program starts: on Plasma's Wayland, test
patches are shown and calibration curves applied through KWin, and KWin's
Night Light is held off while measuring. An instrument that stops responding
is reconnected.

The complete corresponding source of this modified version is available at:

    https://github.com/bson/ArgyllCMS
